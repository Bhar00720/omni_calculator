Frequently Asked Questions

What types of calculations can I perform with Omni Calculator?

Omni Calculator integrates 7 distinct calculation modules: Standard arithmetic, Percentage calculations, Date difference computation, Unit conversions (length, weight, temperature), Health BMI, Financial Loan EMI, and Discount calculations.

How does the Percentage Calculator work?

The Percentage tab provides three dedicated calculators: calculating what X percent of Y is, finding what percentage X is of Y, and calculating the exact percentage increase or decrease between two values.

Can I view previous calculations on the standard calculator?

Yes. Omni Calculator includes a persistent session History Tape that records every calculation and result as you compute them, allowing quick reference and review.

Does Omni Calculator work offline?

Yes. Omni Calculator is built with lightweight, client-side JavaScript. Once loaded, all computations run locally on your device without needing an active internet connection.
